    The Process

    How ClearDrop works

    Water moves through the main filter by gravity. Optional powered disinfection is a separate add-on and requires its own maintenance.

    01

    Water enters

    Rainwater or unsafe water first passes through a coarse intake screen to remove leaves, floating debris, and large particles.

    02

    Layers filter it

    Gravel, sand, activated carbon, and a ceramic filter provide successive filtration stages without electricity in the basic design.

    03

    Clean water is stored

    Filtered water flows into a covered tank. Test water quality and follow local health guidance before using it for drinking.

    About ClearDrop

    A simple gravity filter built for real communities

    ClearDrop was designed to solve a common problem: many families and small communities still rely on unsafe or inconsistent water sources. The system uses natural gravity to clean water without heavy electricity use or expensive machinery.

    We built it because clean water should be practical, affordable, and easy to maintain. With a compact layered design, ClearDrop can provide better water quality for households, schools, and rural community centers.

    • Low-cost filtration system using gravity flow
    • Built with sediment, carbon, ceramic, and storage stages
    • Designed for households, schools, and barangay use
    • Made to support clean water access and sustainability
    Before ordering

    Check your stock and order status

    Material prices and stock counts in this project are sample demo data. Add a material to reserve one piece, then checkout to create an order for the stock manager to review.

    This ordering demo does not certify drinking water as safe. Filter materials must be assembled and validated correctly; test the finished water and follow local public-health guidance before drinking.
