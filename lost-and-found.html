The Process

How claiming works

Three steps, same order every time — so nobody has to guess where their gadget went.

01

It gets logged

Whoever finds an item hands it to the front office or a teacher, who logs it here with the item, location, and date found.

02

You check the board

Search or filter by category above. Each listing shows just enough detail for you to recognize your own gadget, not anyone else's.

03

You claim it in person

Tap "This is mine," then bring your student ID to the front office. Staff verify ownership before releasing any item.

About Us

Run by the Student Council,
backed by the Front Office

Found It started as a corkboard outside Room 101 — now it's the same idea online. Every gadget turned in anywhere on campus gets logged by staff and posted here within the same school day.

We built it because gadgets are expensive and time in line at the office is not. If it's yours, you should be able to spot it from your phone before first period even starts.

  • Managed by the Student Council Welfare Committee
  • Verified in person at the Front Office, Rm 101
  • Unclaimed gadgets held for 30 days, then donated or recycled
  • Open Mon–Fri, 7:00 AM – 4:30 PM
